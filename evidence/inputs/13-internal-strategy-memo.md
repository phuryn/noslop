**Re:** Recommendation to close our three mall stores at lease end and reinvest in Denver and online

**Recommendation**

Let the leases on our three mall stores expire in June 2027 without renewal, and redirect the capital and operating dollars they consume to the Denver flagship and our online business.

**Why now**

Our leases all end in June 2027, which gives us a natural exit with no early-termination penalties. Renewal decisions and landlord negotiations typically start 9 to 12 months before expiration, so the window to decide is the next few months. If we wait, we risk being pulled into renewal terms by default.

**Rationale**

1. **Mall traffic is structurally declining.** The stores depend on foot traffic that we don't control and that has been eroding. Our performance is tied to anchor tenants and mall health rather than to our own brand pull.
2. **The flagship and online are where our brand performs best.** Denver delivers the full brand experience, and online reaches customers regardless of location. Both offer stronger potential returns on incremental investment than the mall stores.
3. **Mall stores are capital- and labor-intensive for what they return.** Rent, CAM charges, staffing, and buildout upkeep for three locations spread management attention and cash thin. Consolidating lets us concentrate talent and inventory.
4. **Flexibility.** Closing at lease end avoids locking in another multi-year commitment in a channel we're already questioning.

**Proposed reinvestment**

- **Denver flagship:** expanded events and programming, enhanced in-store experience, and additional staffing to capture customers from the closing stores.
- **Online:** investment in site performance, fulfillment speed, marketing, and customer retention, with a focus on converting mall-store customers.
- **Customer transition:** targeted outreach to mall-store customers before closing, including promotions, local-pickup or shipping incentives, and an invitation to Denver.

**Risks and mitigations**

- **Lost sales and customers not transferring.** Some mall-store revenue will not follow us. Mitigation: customer data capture and outreach starting at least six months before closing, plus tracking of retention by store.
- **Employee impact.** Mitigation: early communication, redeployment into Denver and online roles where possible, and severance and transition support for others.
- **Concentration risk.** Relying on one physical location and one digital channel raises exposure. Mitigation: set clear performance thresholds and revisit pop-up or wholesale options if needed.
- **Inventory and closing costs.** Mitigation: plan a phased wind-down and liquidation starting in Q1 2027.
