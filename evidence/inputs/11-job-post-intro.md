**Sous Chef – Brasa Norte (Portland, OR)**

We cook over fire, we're slammed on the weekends, and we like it that way. Brasa Norte is looking for a sous chef who can run the line when the chef isn't there, keep the crew steady when tickets stack up, and still care about whether the salt is right on the third plate of the night.

We're open Wednesday through Sunday, dinner only. That means real days off, nights that actually end, and a schedule you can plan a life around. You'll work full time, hands-on, on the line most nights and running the kitchen when it counts.

**What you'll do:** Lead service, run prep, order and receive, train and coach cooks, and keep standards high without turning the kitchen into a pressure cooker. You'll work closely with the chef on menu changes and specials, and you'll have a real say in how we do things.

**What we're after:** Someone who's done this before, ideally with live fire or grill experience, and who's ready for the next step up. You're organized, calm when it gets loud, and the kind of person cooks want to work for.

**Pay:** $68,000–$75,000 a year, depending on experience.

If that sounds like your kind of kitchen, send us a note and tell us where you've cooked and what you're proud of.
