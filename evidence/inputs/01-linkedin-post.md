We cut no-shows at Brightwell Dental Group from 18% to 7% in six months.

The change was simple: we switched to text message reminders across all 9 of our clinics.

Phone calls were hit or miss. Front desk teams spent hours leaving voicemails that rarely got returned. Texts get read, and patients can confirm or reschedule in seconds.

Here's what that meant in practice:

- Fewer empty chairs
- Less time on the phone for our front desk
- More patients getting the care they need, when they need it

Every no-show is a patient who didn't get treated and a slot someone else could have used. Closing that gap matters for both the practice and the people we serve.

The lesson for me: sometimes the biggest operational wins come from meeting people where they already are.

If you run a multi-location practice and are still relying on calls, it's worth testing.
