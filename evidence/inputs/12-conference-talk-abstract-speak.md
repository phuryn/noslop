Finding Leaks with Pressure Data We Already Had

Abstract

Pressure data collected for everyday operations can help reveal leaks that might otherwise go unnoticed. This talk explores how Mesa Valley Water Authority used data it already had to find leaks and help reduce non-revenue water from 14% to 9%. It shares lessons from that work and practical considerations for utilities looking to make better use of their existing pressure data.

Short Bio

A senior engineer at Mesa Valley Water Authority, the speaker helped use existing pressure data to identify leaks and reduce non-revenue water from 14% to 9%.
