**Finding Leaks with Pressure Data We Already Had**

Abstracts Abstract: Water conservation isn't always about investing in new technology or new sensor devices. Mesa Valley Water Authority used existing collected data to help reduce water loss. This talk will focus on how pressure data can be used to help find leaks and the steps we used to take our NRW from 14% to 9%.

Speaker(s) Bio(s) Bio: is a Senior Engineer at Mesa Valley Water Authority. When not traveling the world he is hard at work helping to reduce water loss for MVWA. Most recently using existing pressure data to help reduce MVWA's NRW from 14% to 9%.
