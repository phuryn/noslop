Problem / Opportunity Statement

When a caregiver calls in sick, there are a few steps that need to be taken before the callout can be resolved and a new caregiver is scheduled for the affected visit(s). First, the coordinator needs to determine which upcoming visit(s) are affected, then they need to find out which caregivers might be available to work the visit, and whether they’re qualified to work the visit. Once some options have been identified, the coordinator needs to reach out to one or more potential replacement caregivers, track whether they’ve responded, and finally update the schedule with the new caregiver. This process can take quite a while, especially if there are multiple options to check for availability, and/or if the visit doesn’t start until later that day. Until the process is complete, the visit remains in a somewhat unresolved state, and the coordinator needs to keep track of the status of the callout as they’re working on it.

There’s a lot of manual work involved for the coordinator, and it would be great if there was a more consistent and speedy way of finding a replacement caregiver and confirming that they’re able to work the visit.

Starting in January, Sunrise Home Care will be piloting the auto-reschedule feature, to determine how well it fits in with their scheduling process, and how much it improves the sick call process.

Success will be determined by the following goals:

When a caregiver calls in sick, a replacement caregiver can be found within 30 minutes of the sick call being recorded. How long it takes to find a replacement caregiver will need to be recorded starting when the callout is recorded in the system, and ending when the replacement caregiver is confirmed.

The amount of time that coordinators spend finding replacement caregivers will need to be reduced, compared to when the feature first starts being used.

Coordinators need to be aware of the status of the rescheduling process, and have the ability to intervene if necessary.

Visits can be rescheduled with a caregiver that meets all of the requirements.

This will be determined by how well the above items work for Sunrise Home Care during the pilot, and by how satisfied the coordinators are with the process, and whether there are any aspects of the current process that don’t fit well with how they do scheduling.
