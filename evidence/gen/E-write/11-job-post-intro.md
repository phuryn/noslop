Brasa Norte is seeking a Full Time Sous Chef. We are a dinner only kitchen open Wednesday-Sunday in Portland.

Your job is to work with the chef and the crew to provide a well organized and focused work place to produce great food in a clean manner. The Sous Chef needs to be able to take direction and work with the crew to make sure we are all working together to turn out consistent food. This job requires good communication skills and the ability to keep your head when everyone else is losing theirs. This is not an easy job. The Sous Chef needs to work hard to set an example for the rest of the crew. Good attitude, a work ethic and attention to detail are a must. You need to be able to produce and create a fun environment where everyone wants to come to work every night. This job is not for the faint of heart or someone looking to be a “cowboy” in the kitchen. This is not a desk job. You need to have a love of food and great dedication to doing a good job.

This job is challenging and very rewarding.

This is a full time position and we are offering $68-75k per year depending on experience.
