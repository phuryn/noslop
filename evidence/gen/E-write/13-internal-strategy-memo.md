To: Leadership Team  
Subject: Recommendation on mall stores

I’d recommend that we exit our three mall stores as soon as our leases expire in June 2027 and redirect those resources into our Denver flagship and our online business.

Since the stores are in leases that don’t expire until June 2027, I think it is most responsible to leave the stores open until that time. However, we should not make any long term commitments or spend any money we don’t have to on the stores over the remaining lease time in a way that doesn’t directly contribute to effectively and safely running the stores.

I believe if we redirect all of the funds we would use on the stores into our Denver location and our online business we will be able to create more consistent returns for the company. Additionally, the Denver location is a flagship store and is a great location to have a high impact store, while still being able to interact with local customers, and our online business can reach more customers than the three stores we have in other markets.

To create the most effective transition plan I recommend that the Finance and Retail teams work together to create a detailed financial document that outlines why we are leaving the three stores, how much money we will be saving by leaving the three stores, and how we plan on spending the money we save in our other channels. This will allow us to track our success in closing the stores by giving us a defined set of metrics we can use to show the company that we were successful in closing the stores.

Finally, we need to be careful to navigate this exit in a way that doesn’t damage the business. We will need to think carefully about how we tell customers we are leaving the markets in a way that drives them to either our online business or the Denver store. We’ll also need to carefully consider how we create demand in those markets in the future, and we need to think about how we handle people planning early.

I’d recommend that we approve the exit date of June 2027 and approve a plan to transition out of the stores in the most effective way possible.
