This is my last day as a teacher at Lakeview High. I come in early, like I always have in the past 31 years, to prepare for my last class. Not much has changed: the smell of chemicals, the glassware drying on the rack at the sink, the grooves in the chalkboard from years of use. Many of my students have asked if I'm sad to be leaving, and I've always told them that I'm thankful for the time I've been able to spend here, which is certainly true, but it's not the whole of it. Teaching chemistry has been a very informative profession. It has taught me the importance of change, no matter how slight it may be, and it has taught me patience. With that said, the bell has rung, and I'm going to go turn off the lights and give my keys back.
