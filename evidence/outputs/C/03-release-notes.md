**Ledgerly 4.2 Release Notes**
*Out October 14*

**What's new**

- **Quicker bank syncing:** Your bank feeds now update every 15 minutes, not once an hour, so new transactions turn up much sooner.
- **Edit invoices in bulk:** Need to change a bunch of invoices? You can now do it all at once instead of opening them one at a time.

**Fixes**

- **PDF export:** Currency symbols were showing up wrong in exported PDFs. That's fixed.
