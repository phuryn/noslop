Our no-show rate at Brightwell Dental Group went from 18% to 7% in six months, and all we did was start texting people.

We'd been calling patients to remind them about appointments. It mostly didn't work. My front desk teams spent hours leaving voicemails, and hardly anyone called back. So across all 9 clinics, we moved to text reminders. People actually read them, and they can confirm or reschedule in a few seconds.

The difference showed up quickly. Fewer empty chairs. Less time stuck on the phone for the front desk. More patients getting the care they came in for.

A no-show isn't just a hole in the schedule. It's someone who didn't get treated, and a slot another patient could have used. Closing that gap helps the practice and the people we look after.

What I took from this: a lot of the biggest operational wins come from reaching people where they already are.

If you run a multi-location practice and still rely on phone calls, try texts for a month and see what happens.
