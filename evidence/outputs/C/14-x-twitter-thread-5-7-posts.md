1/6
The Tidewater Bookshop turns 10 this month! We opened in a 400 sq ft space, and now we've got two floors of books. Not bad.

2/6
When you only have 400 sq ft, you make hard choices. Every shelf and table had to earn its spot. We couldn't carry everything, so we started with what we loved.

3/6
Sometimes we think back on that tiny first shop and honestly wonder how we fit it all in. Somehow we did. And then you walked through the door.

4/6
Thank you to everyone who bought a book, told a friend about us, or just came in to wander the shelves. You're the reason we're still here.

5/6
My favorite thing about ten years of bookselling: a book we sold in our first week could still be on someone's shelf right now. Maybe it's been reread a dozen times. Maybe a friend borrowed it and never gave it back.

6/6
Our anniversary party is October 25. Come celebrate with us!

And if you remember the 400 sq ft days, we'd love to hear your stories.
