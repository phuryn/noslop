My last day at Lakeview High, 31 years of teaching chemistry behind me, and I caught myself doing all the usual things. I straightened the lab stools. I checked the taps. I wiped down a bench that was already clean. I'd spent so long treating retirement as a date on a calendar that I never really thought about how I'd walk out of the room. Some lessons had gone beautifully. Others I'd chewed over the whole drive home, trying to work out how to teach them better. There were students I still wondered about, and a few whose questions changed how I explained things for the next twenty years. I was ready to go. I just took my time getting my things together.
