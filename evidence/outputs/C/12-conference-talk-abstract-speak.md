Finding Leaks with Pressure Data We Already Had

Abstract

Most water utilities already collect pressure readings just to keep the system running. At Mesa Valley Water Authority, we found those same readings could point us to leaks nobody knew about. Using data we already had, we helped bring non-revenue water down from 14% to 9%. In this talk, I'll share what worked, what didn't, and what other utilities should think about before digging into their own pressure data.

Short Bio

A senior engineer at Mesa Valley Water Authority, the speaker helped the utility use its existing pressure data to find leaks and cut non-revenue water from 14% to 9%.
