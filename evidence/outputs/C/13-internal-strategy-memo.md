**Re:** Closing our three mall stores when the leases end, and putting the money into Denver and online

**My recommendation**

We should let the leases on our three mall stores run out in June 2027 and not renew them. The money and effort those stores take up should go to the Denver flagship and our online business instead.

**Why we need to decide now**

All three leases end in June 2027, so we can walk away cleanly, with no early-termination penalties. But renewal talks with landlords usually begin 9 to 12 months before expiration, which means we have a few months to decide. If we let it slide, we could end up on renewal terms simply because we didn't act.

**Why I think this is right**

1. **Mall traffic is in long-term decline.** Our mall stores rely on foot traffic we don't control, and it has been shrinking. How they do depends more on anchor tenants and the health of the mall than on how much people want our brand.
2. **Denver and online are where our brand does best.** Denver gives customers the full experience, and online reaches people wherever they are. Extra investment in either should return more than the same money spent on the mall stores.
3. **The mall stores cost a lot for what they bring in.** Rent, CAM charges, staffing, and buildout upkeep across three locations stretch both our cash and our management time. Consolidating lets us focus our people and inventory.
4. **It keeps our options open.** Closing at lease end means we don't sign another multi-year commitment in a channel we're already doubting.

**Where the money would go**

- **Denver flagship:** more events and programming, a better in-store experience, and extra staff to welcome customers coming over from the closing stores.
- **Online:** a faster site, quicker fulfillment, more marketing, and better customer retention, with a particular push to win over mall-store customers.
- **Bringing customers with us:** reaching out to mall-store customers before we close, with promotions, local-pickup or shipping incentives, and an invitation to visit Denver.

**What could go wrong, and what we'd do about it**

- **We lose some sales and customers don't follow.** Some mall revenue won't come with us. We'd start collecting customer data and reaching out at least six months before closing, and track how many customers we keep from each store.
- **Our people are affected.** We'd tell them early, move people into Denver and online roles where we can, and offer severance and transition support to the rest.
- **We'd be more concentrated.** Relying on one physical store and one digital channel adds risk. We'd set clear performance thresholds and look again at pop-ups or wholesale if we need to.
- **Inventory and closing costs.** We'd wind down in phases and start liquidating in Q1 2027.
