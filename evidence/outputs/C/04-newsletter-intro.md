Welcome to issue #112 of The Allotment Letter. It's garlic-planting time! Get it in the ground before the first frost and you'll have something to look forward to next summer. Pick a sunny spot with good drainage, break the bulbs into cloves, and push each one in pointy end up. After that comes the hard bit: leave them alone.
