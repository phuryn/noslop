On my last day at Lakeview High, after 31 years of teaching chemistry, I straightened the lab stools, checked the taps, and wiped down a bench that was already clean. I had spent so long thinking about retirement as a date on the calendar that I hadn't given much thought to how I would actually leave the room. Some lessons had gone beautifully. Others I'd spent the drive home trying to figure out how to teach again. There were students I still wondered about, and a few whose questions had changed the way I explained something for the next twenty years. I was ready to go, but I took my time gathering my things.
