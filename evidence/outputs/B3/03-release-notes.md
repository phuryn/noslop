**Ledgerly 4.2 release notes**
*Releases October 14*

**What's new**

- **Faster bank feed sync:** Bank feeds now sync every 15 minutes instead of hourly, so transactions show up sooner.
- **Bulk invoice editing:** You can update multiple invoices at once instead of one by one.

**Fixes**

- **PDF export:** Currency symbols now display correctly in exported PDFs.
