Finding Leaks with Pressure Data We Already Had

Abstract

Mesa Valley Water Authority already collected pressure data for everyday operations. This talk covers how the Authority used that data to find leaks that might otherwise have gone unnoticed, and how that helped bring non-revenue water down from 14% to 9%. It also covers the lessons from that work and what other utilities should consider before putting their own pressure data to the same use.

Short Bio

The speaker is a senior engineer at Mesa Valley Water Authority and helped use the Authority's existing pressure data to find leaks and reduce non-revenue water from 14% to 9%.
