Text-message reminders took no-shows at Brightwell Dental Group from 18% to 7% in six months. We rolled them out across all 9 of our clinics.

Phone calls were hit or miss. Front desk teams spent hours leaving voicemails that rarely got returned. Texts get read, and patients can confirm or reschedule in seconds. That meant fewer empty chairs, less phone time for the front desk, and more patients getting the care they need when they need it.

Every no-show is a patient who didn't get treated and a slot someone else could have used.

For me, the biggest operational win came from meeting patients where they already are. If you run a multi-location practice and still rely on calls, it's worth testing texts.
