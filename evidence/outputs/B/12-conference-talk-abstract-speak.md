Finding Leaks with Pressure Data We Already Had

Abstract

Mesa Valley Water Authority found leaks using pressure data it was already collecting for everyday operations, and cut non-revenue water from 14% to 9%. This talk covers what the team learned and what other utilities should consider before putting their own pressure data to the same use.

Short Bio

The speaker is a senior engineer at Mesa Valley Water Authority. They helped bring non-revenue water down from 14% to 9% by using the utility's existing pressure data to find leaks.
