1/6
The Tidewater Bookshop turns 10 this month. We opened in a 400 sq ft space, and now we have two floors of books.

2/6
400 sq ft meant making choices. Every shelf, every table, every book had to earn its place. We couldn't stock everything, but we could make a start.

3/6
Sometimes we look back at that first little space and wonder how we ever fit a bookshop inside it. But we did, and you came through the door.

4/6
Thank you to everyone who has bought a book, recommended us to a friend, or stopped in to browse over the past decade. Those visits helped us get here.

5/6
One of the lovely things about ten years in a bookshop is that a book we sold when we first opened might still be sitting on someone's shelf. Maybe it's been reread. Maybe it's been passed along.

6/6
Our anniversary party is October 25. Come celebrate with us.

And if you remember the 400 sq ft days, we'd love to hear your memories.
