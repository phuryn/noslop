Welcome to issue #112 of The Allotment Letter. This week we're planting garlic before the first frost, a small job that gives you something to look forward to next summer. Pick a sunny, well-drained spot, split the bulbs into cloves, and plant each one pointed end up. After that, the hard part is leaving them alone.

A reminder for your diary: the seed swap is Saturday, Oct 18 at Millbrook Community Hall. Bring any spare seeds you'd like to share, and come for a chat and a few ideas for next season.
