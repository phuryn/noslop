Welcome to issue #112 of The Allotment Letter. It's time to plant out your garlic. It's best to get it in the ground before the first frost. Pick a sunny spot that drains well. Break up the bulbs into cloves, then stick them in the ground pointy end up. Then you just need to ignore them until next summer.

Finally, a quick reminder that our seed swap is this Saturday, Oct 18 at the Millbrook Community Hall. If you have some spare seeds, bring them along!
