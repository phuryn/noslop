## What problem are we trying to solve?

When a caregiver calls out for the day (because they are sick, for example), there is a number of visits that need to be rescheduled. Currently, this problem will sit until a coordinator notices that the caregiver isn’t working, and then they will manually try to reschedule the affected visits. This generally involves calling and texting a number of caregeivers to try to find people who are available, qualified, close enough to the visit, and accepted by the client. Once a replacement caregiver has been found, the coordinator needs to make sure that the caregiver and client are okay with the change. This process is very time consuming, and it often happens early in the morning when coordinators need to solve a number of callouts at the same time.

This causes a number of issues, including

- Delayed or missed visits for clients that are dependent on this care

- Inability for coordinators to focus on other tasks

- Variance in how quickly and effectively callouts are handled depending on who is working that day

- Missed visits, decreased client satisfaction, compliance issues, etc. for the agency

Currently there is no automated way of detecting when a caregiver has called out, and handling the issue. Sunrise Home Care has told us that currently they rely on coordinators to handle callouts.

## What are we trying to achieve?

There are a couple main goals that we are shooting for with callout handling

- Automatically reschedule all affected visits within 30 minutes of the callout being reported. Rescheduling is complete either when the new caregiver has confirmed their shift, or the problem has been escalated to a coordinator with an explanation of why the problem couldn’t be handled automatically.

- Significantly decrease the amount of work that coordinators have to do to handle callouts

We will work with Sunrise before our pilot to determine what their current numbers are for these goals, and determine what we should aim to hit. Some potential metrics that we could measure for the above goals are:

|Measure | Goal |

| % of affected visits that are rescheduled within 30 minutes of callout | TBD, aim for 80%+ |

| Median time from callout to affected visits being rescheduled | < 30 minutes |

| Time for coordinator to handle a callout | TBD, aim for 50%+ decrease |

| % of callouts that don’t require any coordinator action | TBD |

| Number of visits that are not covered | No increase |

There are also a number of other goals that we want to hit with this feature

We will be working with Sunrise Home Care in January to run a pilot of our callout handling feature. As part of this, we will work to determine some baseline numbers for the above, and determine if we are hitting our targets of rescheduling in 30 minutes and decreasing coordinator workload.
