This month marks a special milestone for The Tidewater Bookshop: we're ten years old! Ten years ago this month we opened our doors to the public crammed into a little over 400 square feet. Today we occupy two floors and are bursting at the seams with books.

2/6 To say that our little 400 square foot bookshop was small is an understatement.

3/6 We may have two floors now, but we still sometimes pine for that original space.

4/6 We wouldn't be here today without all of you, so thank you!

5/6 One of our favorite things about being in business for ten years is that there are books we sold on day one that are probably still sitting on shelves of books you've purchased from us.

6/6 Join us on October 25 for a fun anniversary party to celebrate 10 years of The Tidewater Bookshop!

If you can remember the little 400 square foot bookshop, we'd love to hear your memories!
