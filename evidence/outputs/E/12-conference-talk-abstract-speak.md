Finding Leaks with Pressure Data We Already Had

Abstract

Abstract Pressure information gathered as part of normal operations can be very helpful in locating leaks. This talk will discuss the process the Mesa Valley Water Authority used to find leaks using pressure data the Authority already had. This effort helped the Authority reduce its NRW from 14% to 9%. Some lessons learned and other items to consider using pressure data to find leaks will also be discussed.
