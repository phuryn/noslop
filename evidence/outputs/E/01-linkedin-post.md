We reduced missed appointments from 18% to 7% in just 6 months!

We did this by implementing text message reminders across all 9 of our clinics in Brightwell Dental Group.

Calling patients to remind them of appointments was hit or miss, most of the time we’d have to leave a voicemail that never got returned. Switching to text messages we’ve found that they get read and patients can confirm or reschedule their appointment in a matter of seconds.

This allows us to

Have fewer missed appointments.

Reduce the amount of time our front desk has to spend calling patients.

Allow more patients to receive the treatment they need.

Missed appointments mean that we are unable to treat a patient that may need treatment and is not utilising a slot that could have been given to another patient.

If you have multiple practices and are still calling patients to remind them of appointments then this is something that you should trial.
