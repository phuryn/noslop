1. Mall traffic is going to continue to go down. We are subject to the whims of mall traffic which is out of our control. We are subject to the success of the anchors in the mall as well.

3. The mall stores are capital and labor intensive and by closing these three locations, it allows us to focus our dollars on the two areas that can help grow the business.

4. It allows us to be flexible in our decision making.
