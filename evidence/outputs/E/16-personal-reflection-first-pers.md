It was my last day teaching at Lakeview High. I had spent 31 years teaching Chemistry to the kids here, and now I was finally leaving. I had carefully prepared for this day, not knowing exactly how I was going to go out, whether with a bang or with a whimper. I had had good years and I had had bad years. I had had many memorable experiences with students both good and bad. Anyway I was finally leaving. I was taking my time cleaning up, and gathering up my things.
