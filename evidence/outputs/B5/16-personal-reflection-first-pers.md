On my last day at Lakeview High, after 31 years of teaching chemistry, I did the usual things. I straightened the lab stools, checked the taps, and wiped down a bench that was already clean. For a long time retirement had been just a date on the calendar, and I'd never thought about how I would actually leave the room. Some lessons had gone beautifully. Others I'd spent the drive home working out how to teach again. There were students I still wondered about, and a few whose questions changed how I explained something for the next twenty years. I was ready to go, but I took my time gathering my things.
