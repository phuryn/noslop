Finding Leaks with Pressure Data We Already Had

Abstract

Utilities collect pressure data to run their systems day to day, and that same data can show leaks that would otherwise go unnoticed. At Mesa Valley Water Authority, we used data we already had to find leaks, and it helped bring non-revenue water down from 14% to 9%. This talk covers what we learned and what other utilities should consider before making more of their own pressure data.

Short Bio

The speaker is a senior engineer at Mesa Valley Water Authority. They helped the utility use pressure data it already had to find leaks, part of the effort that took non-revenue water from 14% to 9%.
