Welcome to issue #112 of The Allotment Letter. This week we're planting garlic before the first frost. It's a small job now, and it gives us something to look forward to next summer. Pick a sunny, well-drained spot, break the bulbs into cloves, and plant each one pointed end up. After that, the hardest part is leaving them alone.

A reminder for your diary: the seed swap is on Saturday, Oct 18, at Millbrook Community Hall. Bring any spare seeds you'd like to share, and come along for a chat and some ideas for next season.
