We cut no-shows at Brightwell Dental Group from 18% to 7% in six months by switching to text message reminders at all 9 of our clinics.

Phone calls were hit or miss. Our front desk teams spent hours leaving voicemails that rarely got returned. Texts get read, and patients can confirm or reschedule in seconds.

Now we have fewer empty chairs, and the front desk spends less time on the phone. More patients get the care they need when they need it. Every no-show is a patient who didn't get treated and a slot someone else could have used, so closing that gap matters for the practice and for the people we serve.

What I took from this is that sometimes the biggest operational wins come from meeting people where they already are. If you run a multi-location practice and are still relying on calls, it's worth testing.
