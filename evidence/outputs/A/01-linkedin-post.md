We cut no-shows at Brightwell Dental Group from 18% to 7% in six months by switching to text message reminders across all 9 of our clinics.

Phone calls weren't working. Front desk teams spent hours leaving voicemails that rarely got returned. Texts get read, and patients can confirm or reschedule in seconds. The result is fewer empty chairs and less phone time for the front desk, and more patients get the care they need when they need it.

Every no-show is a patient who didn't get treated and a slot someone else could have used.

If you run a multi-location practice and still rely on calls, try texts. How are you handling no-shows?
