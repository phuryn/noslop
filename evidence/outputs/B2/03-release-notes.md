**Ledgerly 4.2 Release Notes**
*Releases October 14*

**What's new**

- **Faster bank feed sync:** Bank feeds now sync every 15 minutes instead of hourly, so transactions show up sooner.
- **Bulk invoice editing:** Update multiple invoices at once instead of one by one.

**Fixes**

- **PDF export:** Fixed a bug where currency symbols didn't display correctly in exported PDFs.
