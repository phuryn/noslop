Finding Leaks with Pressure Data We Already Had

Abstract

Pressure data collected for everyday operations can reveal leaks that might otherwise go unnoticed. Mesa Valley Water Authority used data it already had to find leaks, and that helped reduce its non-revenue water from 14% to 9%. This talk covers the lessons from that work and the practical considerations for utilities that want to make better use of their own pressure data.

Short Bio

The speaker is a senior engineer at Mesa Valley Water Authority and helped use the authority's existing pressure data to find leaks and reduce non-revenue water from 14% to 9%.
