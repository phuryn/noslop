Stepwise: Home Exercise for PT

Stepwise gives you home exercises from licensed physical therapists, with a video showing how to do each one.

Why Stepwise

• 300+ video exercises. Every video is filmed by a licensed physical therapist and shows proper form and technique for the movement.

• Reminders. Set a schedule that fits your day and get a nudge when it's time to exercise.

• Progress sharing. Your physical therapist can see how you're doing between visits and adjust your plan.

Who it's for

Stepwise is for people recovering from surgery, managing an injury, or working on strength and mobility. Follow along with each video, check off your exercises, and track your progress over time.
