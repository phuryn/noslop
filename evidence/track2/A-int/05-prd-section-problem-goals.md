## Problem

When a caregiver calls in sick, the visits assigned to them are left uncovered until a coordinator notices, finds a replacement, and updates the schedule by hand. Coordinators typically work through this by phone and text: checking who is available, who is qualified and close enough, and who the client will accept, then confirming with both the caregiver and the client. This takes time, and call-outs often come early in the morning, when several visits are at risk at once.

Clients who depend on help with medication, meals, mobility, or personal care go without it or wait for it when visits are late or missed. Coordinators lose time to reactive work that pulls them away from planning, onboarding, and client communication, and rescheduling is hardest to do well when call-outs cluster. Speed and quality also vary by coordinator, time of day, and call-out volume. For agencies, missed visits put client satisfaction, retention, and compliance at risk, and last-minute scrambling often leads to overtime or poorly matched assignments.

No system currently detects a call-out and acts on it automatically. Sunrise Home Care, our pilot partner, has coordinators handle each case individually.

## Goals

### Primary goals

1. Reschedule affected visits within 30 minutes of a call-out being reported. Rescheduling is complete when a replacement caregiver has confirmed, or when the visit has been escalated to a coordinator with a clear reason.
2. Reduce coordinator manual work on call-out handling. Coordinators should step in only for exceptions.

### Success metrics

Baselines will be measured with Sunrise before the pilot begins, and targets will be confirmed then.

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Share of affected visits rescheduled within 30 minutes | To be set from baseline (proposed: 80%+) |
| Median time from call-out to confirmed replacement | Under 30 minutes |
| Coordinator time spent per call-out | Reduced vs. baseline (proposed: 50%+) |
| Share of call-outs resolved without coordinator action | To be set from baseline |
| Visits left uncovered | No increase vs. baseline |

### Secondary goals

- Preserve match quality: replacements meet qualification, availability, location, and client-preference requirements.
- Keep clients and caregivers informed automatically, so no one has to chase status.
- Give coordinators visibility into what the system did and why, with an easy way to override.

### Pilot scope

The pilot runs with Sunrise Home Care in January. It will establish baselines, validate the 30-minute target, and measure the reduction in coordinator effort. The results will inform the decision on broader rollout.

### Non-goals

- Replacing coordinators or removing their ability to override decisions.
- Long-term schedule optimization or forecasting of call-outs.
- Handling planned absences such as vacations or scheduled leave.
