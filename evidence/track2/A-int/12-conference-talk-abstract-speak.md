Finding Leaks with Pressure Data We Already Had

Abstract

Mesa Valley Water Authority found leaks using pressure data it already collected for everyday operations, and non-revenue water fell from 14% to 9%. The talk covers what the utility learned and what other utilities should weigh before doing the same with their own pressure data.

Short Bio

The speaker is a senior engineer at Mesa Valley Water Authority and helped use that existing data to find leaks and cut non-revenue water from 14% to 9%.
