On my last day at Lakeview High, after 31 years of teaching chemistry, I straightened the lab stools, checked the taps, and wiped down a bench that was already clean. I had thought of retirement for so long as a date on the calendar that I never considered how I would actually leave the room.

What I hadn't planned on was Amina Yusuf turning up during my last class, period 4 AP Chemistry. She's a pharmacist now, and she brought her young daughter to watch. Seeing her made me wonder about all the students like her, where they ended up.

I ended with the elephant toothpaste demo, which I've done every year since my first and which has always gone well. I felt relieved as much as sad, which is a little embarrassing. I have no real plan for what comes next.
