Sous Chef, Brasa Norte (Portland, OR)

We cook over fire and do whole-animal butchery in house. Friday runs about 180 covers, and that's the hardest part of the job. We're looking for a sous chef who can run the line when the chef is out, keep a crew of six cooks steady when tickets stack up, and still care whether the salt is right on the third plate of the night.

We're open Wednesday through Sunday, dinner only, and the job is a four-day week. That means real days off and nights that actually end. You'll work full time, on the line most nights and running the kitchen when the chef isn't there. Health insurance is 100% covered for you.

You'll lead service, run prep, order and receive, train and coach cooks, and keep standards high without turning the kitchen into a pressure cooker. You'll work with the chef on menu changes and specials, and you'll have a say in how we do things.

We want someone who has done this before, ideally with live fire or grill experience, and who's ready for the next step up. You're organized, calm when it gets loud, and the kind of person cooks want to work for.

We promote from within. Our last sous chef stayed four years and left to open her own restaurant. Luis started as a dishwasher and is now a line cook.

Pay is $68,000 to $75,000 a year, depending on experience.

If that sounds like your kind of kitchen, send us a note about where you've cooked and what you're proud of.
