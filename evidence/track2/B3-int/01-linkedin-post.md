We cut no-shows at Brightwell Dental Group from 18% to 7% in six months. Some of that may be summer, when no-shows usually dip anyway, and I don't know whether the drop was steady or came in steps.

We switched to text message reminders across all 9 of our clinics. The system costs $1,400 a month for all of them. I can't name the vendor because our contract forbids it without approval.

Phone calls were hit or miss. Front desk teams left voicemails that rarely got returned. What changed things was two-way texting. Patients can reply to reschedule, and I think that did more than the reminders themselves.

The bigger win, I think, is that front desk staff now save about 11 hours a week on confirmation calls.

Texts don't cover everyone. Joanna, our front desk lead in Gdańsk, noticed that some older patients reply "OK" and still don't show up. Now staff also phone every patient over 70. Sopot only got down to 12%, and nobody is sure why.

If you run a multi-location practice and still rely on calls, it's worth testing. Make sure patients can reply to reschedule, and expect to keep calling some of them.
