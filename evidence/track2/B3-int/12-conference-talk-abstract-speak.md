Finding Leaks with Pressure Data We Already Had

Abstract

Mesa Valley Water Authority found leaks using the SCADA pressure sensors it already had. We bought no new hardware. Over 18 months, non-revenue water went from 14% to 9%, and the project saves about 410 million gallons a year. Total cost was $85,000.

Carlos Ruiz, one of our field techs, used the model's alerts to trace a major main leak under an elementary school. The alerts weren't clean, though. About 30% were false positives in the first months, and other utilities should expect that early on. I also don't think the machine learning was essential. I believe simple threshold rules got most of the gains.

Short Bio

Dr. Naomi Fischer is a Senior Water Systems Engineer at Mesa Valley Water Authority, where she has worked for 12 years. She has a PhD from Arizona State.
