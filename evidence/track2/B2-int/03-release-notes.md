**Ledgerly 4.2 Release Notes**
Releasing October 14

**What's new**

- Faster bank feed sync: Bank feeds now sync every 15 minutes instead of hourly, so your transactions show up sooner.
- Bulk invoice editing: You can now update multiple invoices at once instead of editing them one by one.

**Fixes**

- PDF export: Currency symbols now display correctly in exported PDFs.
