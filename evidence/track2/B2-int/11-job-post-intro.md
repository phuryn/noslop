**Sous Chef, Brasa Norte (Portland, OR)**

We cook over fire, and we do whole-animal butchery in house. Brasa Norte is looking for a sous chef who can run the line when the chef isn't there, keep the crew steady when tickets stack up, and still care whether the salt is right on the third plate of the night.

Friday hits about 180 covers with six cooks on the line, and that's the hardest part of the job. We're open Wednesday through Sunday, dinner only, and you'd work four days a week, full time and hands-on. You'll be on the line most nights and running the kitchen when it counts.

You'll lead service, run prep, order and receive, and train and coach the cooks, keeping standards high without turning the kitchen into a pressure cooker. You'll work closely with the chef on menu changes and specials.

We'd like someone who has done this before, ideally with live fire or grill experience, and who is ready for the next step up. You're organized, calm when it gets loud, and the kind of person cooks want to work for. We promote from within. Luis started as a dishwasher and now cooks on our line, and we're proud of that. Our last sous chef stayed four years and left to open her own place.

Pay is $68,000 to $75,000 a year, depending on experience. Health insurance is 100% covered for the employee.

If that sounds like your kind of kitchen, send us a note and tell us where you've cooked and what you're proud of.
