Welcome to issue #112 of The Allotment Letter. This week we're planting garlic before the first frost. It's a small job now that gives us something to look forward to next summer. Choose a sunny, well-drained spot, separate your bulbs into cloves, and plant them pointed end up. Then comes the hardest part: leaving them alone.

And a reminder for your diary: the seed swap is Saturday, Oct 18 at Millbrook Community Hall. Bring any spare seeds you'd like to share, and come along for a chat and a few ideas for next season.
