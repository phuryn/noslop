## Recommendation

Let the leases on our three mall stores expire in June 2027 without renewal, and redirect the capital and operating dollars they consume to the Denver flagship and our online business.

## Why now

All three leases end in June 2027, so we can exit with no early-termination penalties. Renewal decisions and landlord negotiations typically start 9 to 12 months before expiration, which means we need to decide in the next few months. If we wait, we risk being pulled into renewal terms by default.

## Rationale

1. Mall traffic is structurally declining. The stores depend on foot traffic we don't control, and it has been eroding. Their performance is tied to anchor tenants and mall health rather than to our own brand pull.
2. Denver and online are where our brand performs best. Denver delivers the full brand experience, and online reaches customers regardless of location. Both offer stronger potential returns on incremental investment than the mall stores.
3. The mall stores are capital- and labor-intensive for what they return. Rent, CAM charges, staffing, and buildout upkeep for three locations spread management attention and cash thin. Consolidating lets us concentrate talent and inventory.
4. Closing at lease end keeps us from locking in another multi-year commitment in a channel we're already questioning.

## Proposed reinvestment

- Denver flagship: expanded events and programming, an improved in-store experience, and additional staff to capture customers from the closing stores.
- Online: investment in site performance, fulfillment speed, marketing, and customer retention, focused on converting mall-store customers.
- Customer transition: targeted outreach to mall-store customers before closing, including promotions, local-pickup or shipping incentives, and an invitation to Denver.

## Risks and mitigations

- Lost sales and customers not transferring. Some mall-store revenue will not follow us. Mitigation: capture customer data and start outreach at least six months before closing, and track retention by store.
- Employee impact. Mitigation: communicate early, redeploy people into Denver and online roles where possible, and offer severance and transition support to the rest.
- Concentration risk. Relying on one physical location and one digital channel raises our exposure. Mitigation: set clear performance thresholds and revisit pop-up or wholesale options if needed.
- Inventory and closing costs. Mitigation: plan a phased wind-down and liquidation starting in Q1 2027.
