1/6
The Tidewater Bookshop turns 10 this month. We opened in 400 sq ft. Now we have two floors of books.

2/6
In 400 sq ft we had to be picky about everything. We couldn't stock it all, so we chose carefully. Sometimes we look back at that first space and wonder how we fit a bookshop inside it.

3/6
The first book we sold was a copy of Middlemarch. Mr. Okafor bought it, and he's come in every week since.

4/6
In 2020 we nearly closed, and a $38,000 GoFundMe saved us. Thank you to everyone who gave, and to everyone who has bought a book, recommended us to a friend, or stopped in to browse over the past decade.

5/6
One of the lovely things about ten years in a bookshop is that a book we sold when we first opened might still be sitting on someone's shelf. Maybe it's been reread. Maybe it's been passed along.

6/6
Our anniversary party is October 25. Come celebrate with us.

And if you remember the 400 sq ft days, we'd love to hear your memories.
