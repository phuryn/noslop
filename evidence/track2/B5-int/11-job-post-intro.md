Sous Chef, Brasa Norte (Portland, OR)

We cook over fire, we're slammed on the weekends, and we like it that way. We're looking for a sous chef who can run the line when the chef isn't there, keep the crew steady when tickets stack up, and still care whether the salt is right on the third plate of the night.

We're open Wednesday through Sunday, dinner only, and it's a four-day week. The job is full time and hands-on: you'll be on the line most nights and running the kitchen when it counts. The kitchen is small, with six cooks on the line. Friday is our biggest night at around 180 covers, and it's the hardest part of the job. We also do whole-animal butchery in house.

You'll lead service, run prep, order and receive, train and coach cooks, and keep standards high without turning the kitchen into a pressure cooker. You'll work closely with the chef on menu changes and specials.

We want someone who's done this before, ideally with live fire or grill experience, and who's ready for the next step up. You're organized, calm when it gets loud, and the kind of person cooks want to work for. Our last sous stayed four years and left to open her own place, and we're proud of that. We promote from within, too. Luis started as a dishwasher and is now on the line.

Pay is $68,000 to $75,000 a year, depending on experience. We cover 100% of your health insurance.

If that sounds like your kind of kitchen, send us a note and tell us where you've cooked and what you're proud of.
