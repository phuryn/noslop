Welcome to issue #112 of The Allotment Letter. This week we're planting garlic before the first frost. It's a small job now, and it gives us something to look forward to next summer. Pick a sunny, well-drained spot, separate the bulbs into cloves, and plant them pointed end up. The hardest part comes after that, which is leaving them alone.

Also, put the seed swap in your diary. It's Saturday, Oct 18 at Millbrook Community Hall. Bring any spare seeds you'd like to share, and come along for a chat and a few ideas for next season.
