**Re:** Recommendation to close our three mall stores at lease end and reinvest in Denver and online

**Recommendation**

Let the leases on our three mall stores expire in June 2027 without renewal, and move the capital and operating dollars they use to the Denver flagship and our online business.

**Why now**

All three leases end in June 2027, so we can exit without early-termination penalties. Renewal decisions and landlord negotiations typically start 9 to 12 months before expiration, which means we need to decide in the next few months. If we wait, we risk being pulled into renewal terms by default.

**Rationale**

1. Mall traffic is declining structurally. The stores depend on foot traffic we don't control, and it has been eroding. Their performance follows anchor tenants and mall health more than our own brand pull.
2. Denver and online are where our brand performs best. Denver delivers the full brand experience, and online reaches customers wherever they are. Both offer stronger potential returns on added investment than the mall stores do.
3. For what they return, the mall stores take a lot of capital and labor. Rent, CAM charges, staffing, and buildout upkeep across three locations spread management attention and cash thin. Consolidating lets us concentrate talent and inventory.
4. Closing at lease end keeps us from locking into another multi-year commitment in a channel we're already questioning.

**Proposed reinvestment**

- Denver flagship: expanded events and programming, an enhanced in-store experience, and additional staffing to capture customers from the closing stores.
- Online: investment in site performance, fulfillment speed, marketing, and customer retention, with a focus on converting mall-store customers.
- Customer transition: targeted outreach to mall-store customers before closing, including promotions, local-pickup or shipping incentives, and an invitation to Denver.

**Risks and mitigations**

- Lost sales and customers not transferring. Some mall-store revenue won't follow us. To limit that, we'd start capturing customer data and doing outreach at least six months before closing, and track retention by store.
- Employee impact. We'd communicate early, redeploy people into Denver and online roles where possible, and offer severance and transition support to the others.
- Concentration risk. Relying on one physical location and one digital channel raises our exposure. We'd set clear performance thresholds and revisit pop-up or wholesale options if needed.
- Inventory and closing costs. We'd plan a phased wind-down and liquidation starting in Q1 2027.
