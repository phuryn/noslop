We cut no-shows at Brightwell Dental Group from 18% to 7% in six months, after moving all 9 of our clinics to a two-way text system. It costs about $1,400 a month for the whole group.

It wasn't just "send reminders." Patients can reschedule by replying to the text, and that two-way part is what made the difference.

The old confirmation calls ate a lot of front desk time. We now save about 11 hours a week, and I honestly think that's the bigger win. Every no-show is also a patient who didn't get treated and a slot someone else could have used.

It isn't a magic fix. Joanna at our Gdańsk clinic noticed that elderly patients would reply "OK" and still not show up, so we now also phone everyone over 70. Sopot only got to 12%, and nobody's sure why. I also worry a bit that our six months included summer, when no-shows tend to drop anyway, so the texting may not get all the credit.

If you run a multi-location practice and still rely on calls, it's worth testing.
