On my last day at Lakeview High, after 31 years of teaching chemistry, I straightened the lab stools, checked the taps, and wiped down a bench that was already clean. My last class was period 4 AP Chemistry, and I closed with the elephant toothpaste demo, which I've done every year since my first. Amina Yusuf was on my mind that day because she showed up with her young daughter to watch. She's a pharmacist now. I felt relieved as much as sad, and I'm a little embarrassed by that. I don't have a real plan for what comes next. I took my time gathering my things.
