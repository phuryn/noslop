Sous Chef, Brasa Norte (Portland, OR)

We cook over fire and we're slammed on weekends, which suits us. Brasa Norte needs a sous chef who can run the line when the chef is out, keep the crew steady when tickets stack up, and still care whether the salt is right on the third plate of the night.

We're open Wednesday through Sunday, dinner only, so you get real days off and a schedule you can plan around. The job is full time and hands-on. You'll be on the line most nights and running the kitchen when the chef isn't there.

You'll lead service, run prep, order and receive, train and coach cooks, and keep standards high without making the kitchen tense. You'll work with the chef on menu changes and specials, and you'll have a real say in how we do things.

We want someone who has done this before, ideally with live fire or grill experience, and is ready for the next step up. You're organized, calm when it gets loud, and a person cooks want to work for.

Pay: $68,000–$75,000 a year, depending on experience.

If that sounds like your kind of kitchen, send us a note about where you've cooked and what you're proud of.
