We cut no-shows at Brightwell Dental Group from 18% to 7% in six months by switching reminders from phone calls to text messages at all 9 of our clinics.

Calls were hit or miss. Front desk teams spent hours leaving voicemails that rarely got returned. Texts get read, and patients can confirm or reschedule in seconds, so we have fewer empty chairs and the front desk spends less time on the phone.

Every no-show is a patient who didn't get treated and a slot someone else could have used. For me, the biggest operational win came from using the channel patients already pay attention to.

If you run a multi-location practice and still rely on calls, test texts.
