**Re:** Recommendation to close our three mall stores at lease end and reinvest in Denver and online

**Recommendation**

Let the leases on our three mall stores expire in June 2027 without renewal, and redirect the capital and operating dollars they consume to the Denver flagship and our online business.

**Why now**

All three leases end in June 2027, so we can exit without early-termination penalties. Renewal decisions and landlord negotiations typically start 9 to 12 months before expiration, which means we need to decide in the next few months. If we wait, we may be pulled into renewal terms by default.

**Rationale**

1. Mall traffic has been declining, and we don't control it. The stores depend on that foot traffic, and their performance is tied more to anchor tenants and mall health than to our own brand pull.
2. Our brand performs best in the Denver flagship and online. Denver delivers the full brand experience, and online reaches customers regardless of location. Both should return more on incremental investment than the mall stores.
3. The mall stores take a lot of capital and labor for what they return. Rent, CAM charges, staffing, and buildout upkeep across three locations spread management attention and cash thin. Consolidating lets us concentrate talent and inventory.
4. Closing at lease end keeps us from locking into another multi-year commitment in a channel we're already questioning.

**Proposed reinvestment**

- Denver flagship: expanded events and programming, an enhanced in-store experience, and additional staffing to capture customers from the closing stores.
- Online: investment in site performance, fulfillment speed, marketing, and customer retention, focused on converting mall-store customers.
- Customer transition: targeted outreach to mall-store customers before closing, including promotions, local-pickup or shipping incentives, and an invitation to Denver.

**Risks and mitigations**

- Lost sales and customers not transferring. Some mall-store revenue will not follow us. Mitigation: customer data capture and outreach starting at least six months before closing, plus tracking of retention by store.
- Employee impact. Mitigation: early communication, redeployment into Denver and online roles where possible, and severance and transition support for others.
- Concentration risk. Relying on one physical location and one digital channel raises our exposure. Mitigation: set clear performance thresholds and revisit pop-up or wholesale options if needed.
- Inventory and closing costs. Mitigation: a phased wind-down and liquidation starting in Q1 2027.
