# Tell lists (supporting file for SKILL.md)

Lists are for the mechanical sweep in Final checks step 2. A single hit is weak evidence; several in one passage are the tell. Leave a word alone inside a quotation, a product name, or a passage that discusses the word.

## Stock AI vocabulary

Built the conservative way: words from the public AI-tell literature that appear **zero times** in a 227-post archive of one professional writer's newsletter (2022-2026). A hit is drift, not style.

## Pause-and-point and staged candor

Here's the thing / Here's what matters / Here's what you need to know / Here's why this matters / Here's the kicker / Let me be clear / Let me explain / Let's break it down / Let's dive in / What nobody tells you / The real story is / One thing trips people up / The truth is / Honestly? / The best part: ... / The secret: ...

## Restating and engagement closers

The bottom line / Key takeaway / TL;DR at the end of a short text / That's the real win / That's the power of X / And that's why X is the future / The future is X / What do you think? / Thoughts? / Agree? / Let that sink in / Read that again

## Filler hedges (cut everywhere)

arguably, it's worth noting, it's important to note, it could potentially, it may be possible that, to some extent, in many ways, I think maybe

## Op-ed attribution verbs

posits, contends, asserts, maintains that, concedes, argues that (for a plain statement). Use says, writes, found, reports.

## Em dash substitutes by context

| Context | Substitute | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Label then description | Colon | "Exports: CSV and PDF" |
| Two complete thoughts | Period | "We cut the price. Usage doubled." |
| A real aside | Parentheses | "the three admins (two in Berlin) approved it" |
| A light pause inside one thought | Comma | "The fix, shipped Tuesday, cut errors by half." |
| Never | Semicolon, or a colon that sets up a reveal | |
