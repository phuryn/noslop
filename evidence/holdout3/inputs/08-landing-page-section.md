## How it works

Get a clear view of crop health across your fields for £6 per hectare. Available in Lincolnshire and Norfolk, with a minimum booking of 40 hectares.

### 1. Book your survey
Tell us where your fields are and how many hectares you’d like surveyed.

### 2. We fly over your fields
We survey your crops by drone, capturing images to build your crop health maps.

### 3. Get your maps within 48 hours
Receive your crop health maps within 48 hours of the flight to help you spot areas that need a closer look.
