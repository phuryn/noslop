We've just passed 1,000 tonnes of textiles kept out of landfill this year at ReThread Mills.

It's a number I'm proud of, but I want to be clear about where it comes from. It comes from the sorting team at our Bradford site.

We only opened Bradford in March 2023. Since then, the team has gone through bag after bag of clothing and fabric by hand, deciding what can be worn again, what can be reused, and what can be recycled into something new. It's careful, repetitive work, and it rarely gets noticed. But every tonne in that 1,000 passed through someone's hands first.

To everyone on the Bradford team: thank you. Your skill, patience and care are the reason this milestone happened. I'm grateful to work alongside you.

I'm also grateful to everyone who donated, partnered with us, or simply believed a new site could make a difference.

There's still a lot of work ahead, and 1,000 tonnes is a small part of the textile waste out there. But it's a good moment to pause and say thank you.
