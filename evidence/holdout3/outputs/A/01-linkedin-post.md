We've just passed 1,000 tonnes of textiles kept out of landfill this year at ReThread Mills.

I'm proud of that number, and it belongs to the sorting team at our Bradford site.

We only opened Bradford in March 2023. Since then, the team has gone through bag after bag of clothing and fabric by hand, deciding what can be worn again, what can be reused, and what can be recycled into something new. It's careful, repetitive work that rarely gets noticed, but every tonne in that 1,000 passed through someone's hands first.

To everyone on the Bradford team: thank you. This milestone happened because of your skill and patience, and I'm grateful to work alongside you.

Thank you also to everyone who donated, partnered with us, or believed a new site could make a difference.

There's still a lot of work ahead, and 1,000 tonnes is a small fraction of the textile waste out there.
