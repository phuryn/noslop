## How it works

Crop health surveys cost £6 per hectare, with a minimum booking of 40 hectares. We cover Lincolnshire and Norfolk.

### 1. Book your survey
Tell us where your fields are and how many hectares you'd like surveyed.

### 2. We fly over your fields
A drone photographs your crops, and we use the images to build your crop health maps.

### 3. Get your maps within 48 hours
We count the 48 hours from the flight. The maps show which areas need a closer look.
