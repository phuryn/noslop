## How it works

We survey your fields by drone and send you crop health maps. It's £6 per hectare, with a minimum booking of 40 hectares, and we cover Lincolnshire and Norfolk.

### 1. Book your survey
Tell us where your fields are and how many hectares you want surveyed.

### 2. We fly your fields
We fly a drone over your crops and take images, which we use to build your crop health maps.

### 3. Get your maps within 48 hours
Your maps reach you within 48 hours of the flight, and they help you spot areas that need a closer look.
