We've just passed 1,000 tonnes of textiles kept out of landfill this year at ReThread Mills. I'm proud of that number, and it belongs to the sorting team at our Bradford site.

We only opened Bradford in March 2023. Since then the team has gone through bag after bag of clothing and fabric by hand, deciding what can be worn again, what can be reused, and what can be recycled into something new. It's careful, repetitive work and it rarely gets noticed, but every tonne of that 1,000 passed through someone's hands first.

Thank you to everyone on the Bradford team. Your skill, patience and care are why we got here, and I'm grateful to work alongside you.

Thanks also to everyone who donated, partnered with us, or simply believed a new site could make a difference.

There's still a lot of work ahead, and 1,000 tonnes is a small part of the textile waste out there. But it's a good moment to pause and say thank you.
