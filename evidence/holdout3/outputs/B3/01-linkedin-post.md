We've just passed 1,000 tonnes of textiles kept out of landfill this year at ReThread Mills. I'm proud of that number, and it belongs to the sorting team at our Bradford site.

We only opened Bradford in March 2023. Since then the team has gone through bag after bag of clothing and fabric by hand, deciding what can be worn again, what can be reused, and what can be recycled into something new. The work is careful and repetitive, and it rarely gets noticed. Every one of those 1,000 tonnes passed through someone's hands first.

To everyone on the Bradford team: thank you. Your skill, patience and care are the reason we got here, and I'm grateful to work alongside you.

Thank you also to everyone who donated, partnered with us, or simply believed a new site could make a difference.

There's still a lot of work ahead, and 1,000 tonnes is a small part of the textile waste out there.
