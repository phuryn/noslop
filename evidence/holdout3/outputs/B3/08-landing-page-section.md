## How it works

Crop health surveys cost £6 per hectare. We cover Lincolnshire and Norfolk, with a minimum booking of 40 hectares.

### 1. Book your survey
Tell us where your fields are and how many hectares you want surveyed.

### 2. We fly over your fields
We fly a drone over your crops and build the images into crop health maps.

### 3. Get your maps within 48 hours
The maps reach you within 48 hours of the flight, to help you spot areas that need a closer look.
