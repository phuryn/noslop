## How it works

See how your crops are really doing, field by field, for £6 a hectare. We cover Lincolnshire and Norfolk, and bookings start at 40 hectares.

### 1. Book your survey
Let us know where your fields are and how much you'd like surveyed.

### 2. We fly your fields
We send a drone over your crops and capture the images we need to build your crop health maps.

### 3. Maps in your inbox within 48 hours
Within 48 hours of the flight, you'll have your maps in hand, ready to show you which patches deserve a closer look.
