We've just passed 1,000 tonnes of textiles kept out of landfill this year at ReThread Mills.

I'm proud of that number, but it isn't mine to take credit for. It belongs to the sorting team at our Bradford site.

We only opened Bradford in March 2023. Since then, the team has worked through bag after bag of clothing and fabric by hand, deciding what can be worn again, what can be reused, and what can be recycled into something new. It's slow, repetitive work, and most people never see it. But every one of those 1,000 tonnes went through someone's hands first.

To the Bradford team: thank you. Your skill, patience and care made this happen, and I'm lucky to work alongside you.

Thank you as well to everyone who donated, partnered with us, or just believed a new site could make a difference.

There's plenty still to do, and 1,000 tonnes is a small slice of the textile waste out there. But it feels right to stop for a moment and say thanks.
