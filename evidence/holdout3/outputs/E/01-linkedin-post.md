We hit a really special milestone today at ReThread Mills.

I wanted to take a moment to recognise the hard work and dedication of the sorting team at our Bradford site. Without them, we wouldn’t have been able to keep over 1000 tonnes of textiles out of landfill this year.

Since opening the site in March 2023, they have painstakingly sorted bag after bag of clothes and fabrics, making sure we find the best use for as much of it as possible – whether that’s for reuse or recycling. It’s hard, repetitive work, but it’s incredibly important work, and it rarely gets the recognition it deserves.

So, to the team in Bradford – thank you. I’m really proud of what we’ve achieved together and I’m so grateful to be able to work alongside such a talented, patient and careful team.

And to everyone who has helped the team achieve this, whether by donating textiles, partnering with us or simply by believing in us and our vision for the new site, thank you.

We’ve got a way to go yet, but it’s nice to take a moment to reflect and say thank you.
