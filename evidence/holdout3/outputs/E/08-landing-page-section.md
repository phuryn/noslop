How do you get your crop maps?

Want to know how healthy your crops are? For just £6 per hectare, we can fly over your fields and provide you with some valuable insight. This service is currently available in Lincolnshire and Norfolk and the minimum area we will fly is 40 hectares.

1. Book your crop health survey

Simply let us know where your fields are and how many hectares you want us to fly over.

2. Sit back as we fly over your fields

We’ll fly over your crops using our drone and collect the images we need to create your crop health maps.

3. Receive your maps within 48 hours

Within 48 hours you will receive your crop health maps.
