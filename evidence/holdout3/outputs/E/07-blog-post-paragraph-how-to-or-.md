How I, a professional translator, feel about MT? I've been working as a sworn translator (es>en) for 12 years, mostly translating legal documents (contracts, agreements, employment contracts, etc) and several language agencies always try to give me machine translated documents to "post-edit", with the excuse that they pay less (€ 0.04 / word) . But what they don't know is that it's NOT cheaper. When it comes to legal documents, and specially contracts, editing a MT text is exactly the same as translating it, as the translator will have to check every single word in the original text ,as the MT can sound fluent but fluent and correct are not synonym, especially in the legal field, where a mistake can mean a big difference in terms of meaning. Not to mention the false friends that the MT doesn't translate. So the translator will have to read the original text, then the MT and if it is wrong he'll translate it. It's just the same work, but being paid at 1/3. And it's NOT cheaper for the client either, as the bill that he'll have to pay when he's sued because of a bad translation will be way more expensive than if he had paid a good translation in the first place.
