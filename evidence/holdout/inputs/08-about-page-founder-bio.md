I’m Mateo Ruiz, founder of Ruiz Piano Restoration. I started the business in Asheville in 2011 after apprenticing at Steinway in Queens, and we’ve restored more than 400 pianos since then.

What I love about this work is that every piano has a history—and a future worth caring for. It’s a privilege to help these instruments find their voice again and bring music into people’s homes for years to come.
