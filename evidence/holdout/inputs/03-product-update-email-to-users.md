Subject: New update for your Kestrel Aura hearing aids

Hello,

We have a free update coming for your Kestrel Aura hearing aids. It's called firmware 3.4, and it brings two improvements:

**Crowd Focus mode.** Restaurants, family gatherings, and busy shops can make it hard to follow a conversation. Crowd Focus helps your hearing aids bring the voice in front of you forward, so the people you're talking with are easier to hear.

**Longer battery life.** Your hearing aids will now run longer on each charge, so you can go further between charges.

**When and how you'll get it**

The update begins rolling out on October 20 through the Kestrel app. When it's ready for you, the app will let you know. Just open the app and follow the steps on the screen. Keep your hearing aids close to your phone while the update installs.

Not sure how to get started? Our support team is happy to walk you through it, step by step. You can reach us at any time, and there's no such thing as a silly question.

Thank you for being part of the Kestrel family.
