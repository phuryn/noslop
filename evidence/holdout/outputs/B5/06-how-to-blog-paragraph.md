Set the tent up somewhere dry and well ventilated. Clean the seam and let it dry completely. If the old seam sealer is loose or peeling, gently pull it off.

Before you apply anything, check that your Seam Grip formula works with your tent's fabric. Then spread a thin, even layer of Gear Aid Seam Grip along the stitching, covering the needle holes and a little of the fabric on either side.

Keep the temperature at or above 50°F while it cures, and give it 24 hours before you pack the tent or expose it to rain. When it's cured, spray the seam with water and check inside for leaks.
