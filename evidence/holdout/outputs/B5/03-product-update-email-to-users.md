Subject: New update for your Kestrel Aura hearing aids

Hello,

We're releasing a free update for your Kestrel Aura hearing aids, firmware 3.4. It adds a new Crowd Focus mode and makes your battery last longer on each charge.

Crowd Focus is for places like restaurants, family gatherings, and busy shops, where following a conversation can be hard. It helps your hearing aids bring the voice in front of you forward, so the people you're talking with are easier to hear.

The update starts rolling out on October 20 through the Kestrel app. When it's ready for you, the app will let you know. Open the app and follow the steps on the screen, and keep your hearing aids close to your phone while the update installs.

If you're not sure how to get started, our support team can walk you through it step by step. You can reach us any time, and no question is too small.

Thanks for being part of the Kestrel family.
