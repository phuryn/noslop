To reseal a leaky tent seam, pitch the tent somewhere dry and well ventilated, then clean the seam and let it dry completely. Gently remove any loose, peeling seam sealer and apply a thin, even layer of Gear Aid Seam Grip along the stitching, covering the needle holes and a little fabric on either side. Check that your Seam Grip formula is compatible with your tent's fabric before applying it. Keep the temperature at or above 50°F, and let the sealant cure for 24 hours before packing the tent or exposing it to rain. Once cured, spray the seam with water and check inside for leaks.
