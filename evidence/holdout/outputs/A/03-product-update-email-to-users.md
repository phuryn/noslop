Subject: New update for your Kestrel Aura hearing aids

Hello,

A free update for your Kestrel Aura hearing aids is coming. Firmware 3.4 adds two improvements.

Crowd Focus mode makes conversation easier to follow in restaurants, at family gatherings, and in busy shops. It brings the voice in front of you forward, so the people you're talking with are easier to hear.

The update also gives your hearing aids longer battery life, so they run longer on each charge.

The rollout begins October 20 through the Kestrel app. When the update is ready for you, the app will let you know. Open the app, follow the steps on the screen, and keep your hearing aids close to your phone while the update installs.

If you'd like help getting started, our support team can walk you through it step by step. You can reach us at any time.
