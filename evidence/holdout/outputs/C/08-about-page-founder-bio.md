I'm Mateo Ruiz, and I run Ruiz Piano Restoration in Asheville. I started it in 2011, after apprenticing at Steinway in Queens. Since then we've restored more than 400 pianos.

I love this work because every piano has a past, and it's worth looking after what comes next. It's a real privilege to help these instruments sound like themselves again and to put music back in people's homes for years to come.
