Subject: A free update for your Kestrel Aura hearing aids

Hi,

We're rolling out a free update for your Kestrel Aura hearing aids. It's firmware 3.4, and it does two things:

**Crowd Focus mode.** Restaurants, family get-togethers, and busy shops can make conversation hard to follow. Crowd Focus brings the voice of the person in front of you forward, so they're easier to hear.

**Longer battery life.** Your hearing aids will now last longer on each charge, so you can go further before plugging in.

**How you'll get it**

The update starts rolling out on October 20 through the Kestrel app. When it's ready for you, the app will let you know. Open it and follow the steps on the screen. Just keep your hearing aids near your phone while it installs.

Not sure where to start? Our support team will gladly walk you through it. Reach out any time. No question is too small.

Thanks for being part of the Kestrel family.
