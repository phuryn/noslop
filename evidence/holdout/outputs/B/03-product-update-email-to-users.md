Subject: Firmware 3.4 for your Kestrel Aura hearing aids, free from October 20

Hello,

Firmware 3.4, a free update for your Kestrel Aura hearing aids, starts rolling out on October 20. It adds two things.

Crowd Focus mode brings the voice in front of you forward, which makes the people you're talking with easier to hear in restaurants, at family gatherings, and in busy shops. Your hearing aids will also run longer on each charge.

The update arrives through the Kestrel app, which will tell you when it's ready for you. Open the app, follow the steps on the screen, and keep your hearing aids close to your phone while the update installs.

If you'd like help getting started, our support team will walk you through it step by step. You can reach them at any time.

Thank you for choosing Kestrel.
