To reseal a leaky tent seam, pitch the tent somewhere dry and well ventilated, clean the seam, and let it dry completely. Check that your Seam Grip formula works with your tent's fabric. Then gently remove any loose, peeling seam sealer and apply a thin, even layer of Gear Aid Seam Grip along the stitching, covering the needle holes and a little fabric on either side. Keep the temperature at or above 50°F, and let the sealant cure for 24 hours before you pack the tent or expose it to rain. Once it's cured, spray the seam with water and check inside for leaks.
