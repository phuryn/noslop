Subject: Free update for your Kestrel Aura hearing aids

Hello,

Firmware 3.4, a free update for your Kestrel Aura hearing aids, starts rolling out on October 20 through the Kestrel app. It adds two things:

- Crowd Focus mode: in restaurants, at family gatherings, and in busy shops, it helps your hearing aids bring the voice in front of you forward, so the people you're talking with are easier to hear.
- Longer battery life: your hearing aids will run longer on each charge.

When the update is ready for you, the app will let you know. Open the app and follow the steps on the screen, and keep your hearing aids close to your phone while it installs.

If you'd like help, our support team can walk you through it step by step. You can reach us at any time, and no question is too small.

Thank you for being part of the Kestrel family.
