A prepaid funeral plan can feel like a kind and sensible thing to do, and for some families it is. It isn't right for everyone, though, so think carefully before you sign.

The average funeral in Norfolk costs about £4,200. A plan bought today may only cover certain services, which can leave extras like flowers, catering or a venue to pay for later. Plans can also be hard to change if your wishes shift, you move away, or the provider changes its terms, and getting a refund isn't always simple.

Some people are better off putting money aside in an account they control, or writing down their wishes and talking them through with family. None of that means a plan is wrong for you. Just read the small print closely first.
