Since March, our Avonmouth plant has been running a new tunnel washer, and the drop in water use has been bigger than we expected.

We now process 14 tonnes of hospital linen a day with far less water than before. At a site this size, that matters for the environment and for our running costs, and it means we can keep up with demand without the same strain on resources.

A machine doesn't do that on its own. Bedding, theatre gowns and towels have to be clean, safe and back on the ward on time, and that takes people who care about getting it right.

To our night shift team: thank you. You've taken on new equipment, new routines and new ways of working with patience and good humour, often while most of us are asleep. The results we're seeing are down to you.

There's more to do and we'll keep looking for ways to improve, but it's good to stop and recognise progress when it happens. Thank you to everyone involved.
