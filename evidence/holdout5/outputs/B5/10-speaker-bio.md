They're a consultant prosthetist at Limbwise in Sheffield, with 15 years of experience. At the ISPO UK conference on 12 November, they'll give a talk called "Fitting Children Who Keep Growing" on the practical challenges of fitting prostheses for children as they grow. As children grow, their needs change, so fit isn't settled in one appointment. It stays something to think about. Drawing on their clinical experience, they'll talk about what that means for prosthetic care and the decisions involved in supporting children through those changes, with comfort, function and everyday life at the centre.
