A prepaid funeral plan can feel like a thoughtful, sensible thing to do, and for some families it is. But it isn't right for everyone, so take your time before you sign anything. A funeral in Norfolk costs around £4,200 on average, yet a plan you buy today might only cover certain services. Things like flowers, catering or a venue could be left for your family to pay for later. Plans can also be awkward to change if your wishes shift, you move away, or the provider alters its terms, and getting your money back isn't always straightforward. Some people are better off setting money aside in an account they control, or writing down their wishes and talking them through with their family. None of this means a plan is wrong for you.
