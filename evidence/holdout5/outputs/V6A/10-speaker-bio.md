They've been a consultant prosthetist at Limbwise in Sheffield for 15 years. On 12 November, they'll be speaking at the ISPO UK conference. The talk, "Fitting Children Who Keep Growing", looks at the practical challenges of fitting prostheses for children as they grow. Because a child's needs keep changing, getting the fit right isn't a one-off appointment but something that needs attention over time. Drawing on their clinical experience, they'll talk about what that means for prosthetic care and the decisions involved in supporting children through these changes, always keeping comfort, function and everyday life at the heart of the conversation.
