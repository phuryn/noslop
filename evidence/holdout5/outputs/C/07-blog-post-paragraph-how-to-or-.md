A prepaid funeral plan can feel like the kind, sensible thing to do, and for some families it is. But it isn't right for everyone, so think it through before you sign.

The average funeral in Norfolk costs about £4,200. A plan bought today might only cover certain services, which can leave you or your family paying later for things like flowers, catering or a venue. Plans can also be hard to change if your wishes shift, you move away, or the provider alters its terms. Getting a refund isn't always straightforward either.

Some people do better putting money aside in an account they control, or simply writing down their wishes and talking them over with family.

None of this means a plan is wrong for you. It just deserves a close read of the small print first.
