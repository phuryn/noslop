A consultant prosthetist at Limbwise in Sheffield, they've spent 15 years fitting prostheses. On 12 November they'll speak at the ISPO UK conference in a talk called "Fitting Children Who Keep Growing". Children's needs change as they grow, so a good fit isn't something you sort out in one appointment. It keeps needing attention. They'll draw on their own clinical experience to talk about what that means for prosthetic care and the decisions involved in supporting children through each stage, always keeping comfort, function and everyday life at the heart of it.
