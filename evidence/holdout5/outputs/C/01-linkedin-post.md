Our Avonmouth plant has had a new tunnel washer since March, and the drop in water use has surprised even us.

We're getting through 14 tonnes of hospital linen a day on a lot less water than before. At this scale, that's a big deal. It's good for the environment, it eases our running costs, and we can meet demand without piling more pressure on resources.

But a machine turning up doesn't make any of that happen. Bedding, theatre gowns and towels have to be clean, safe and back on the ward on time, and that comes down to people who care about getting it right.

So a special thank you to our night shift. You've taken on new equipment, new routines and new ways of working, with patience and good humour, usually while the rest of us are asleep. These results are yours.

There's still more to do, and we'll keep looking for ways to get better. For now, it's worth stopping to say well done.

Thank you, all of you.
