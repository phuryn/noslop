A desk for you and a crèche spot for your little one, both in a single booking on MacCurtain Street.

### 1. Pick your half day
Choose mornings (9am to 1pm) or afternoons (1pm to 5pm). You book the desk and the childcare together, so there's nothing else to sort out.

### 2. Come in together
Drop your little one off at the crèche, then head to your desk. You're both under the same roof, with time for work and time for care.

### 3. Get your head down
That's four hours to focus on what needs doing. A half day is €28, desk and crèche included, so you know the price before you book.
