We've been running a new tunnel washer at our Avonmouth plant since March, and the drop in water use has surprised us. It's bigger than we expected.

We're now getting through 14 tonnes of hospital linen a day on much less water than before. At a site this size, that counts. It's good for the environment, it helps keep our running costs down, and it means we can meet demand without putting the same pressure on resources.

But a machine turning up doesn't make this happen on its own. Bedding, theatre gowns and towels have to be clean, safe and back on the ward on time, and that depends on people who care about getting it right.

A special thank you to our night shift team. New equipment, new routines, new ways of working: you've taken it all on with patience and good humour, often while the rest of us are asleep. The results we're seeing are down to you.

There's more to do, and we'll keep looking for ways to get better. For now, though, it's worth stopping to recognise the progress we've made.

Thank you to everyone involved.
