Book a desk and a crèche place for your little one together on MacCurtain Street.

### 1. Pick your half day
Choose a morning session from 9am to 1pm or an afternoon session from 1pm to 5pm. The desk and the childcare slot are booked in one go.

### 2. Come along together
Bring your little one to the crèche, then settle in at your desk. You'll both be in the same place.

### 3. Get on with your work
You have four hours to work. Each half day costs €28, with the desk and the crèche included, and you see the price before you book.
