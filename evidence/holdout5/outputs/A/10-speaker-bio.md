With 15 years of experience, they are a consultant prosthetist at Limbwise in Sheffield. Their talk at the ISPO UK conference on 12 November, "Fitting Children Who Keep Growing", will cover the practical challenges of fitting prostheses for children as they grow. Because children's needs change as they grow, fit has to be checked repeatedly and is not settled at a single appointment. They will draw on their clinical experience to discuss what this means for prosthetic care and the decisions involved in supporting children through those changes, keeping comfort, function and everyday life in view.
