Since March, our Avonmouth plant has been running a new tunnel washer, and our water use has dropped by more than we expected.

We now process 14 tonnes of hospital linen every day with far less water than before. That's better for the environment and cuts running costs, and it lets us keep up with demand with less strain on resources.

A machine doesn't do that alone. Bedding, theatre gowns and towels have to be clean, safe and back on the ward on time, and that takes people who care about getting it right.

Thank you to our night shift team. You've taken on new equipment, routines and ways of working with patience and good humour, often while most of us are asleep. The results are down to you.

There's more to do, and we'll keep looking for ways to improve. Thank you to everyone involved.
