Since March, we’ve been running a new tunnel washer at our Avonmouth plant. It uses significantly less water.

It might not sound like much, but when you’re washing 14 tonnes of linen for hospitals every day, making a change like this can make a big difference – both to the environment and to our business.

But our quality of service doesn’t just come from our machinery. The clean, safe laundry we return to our customers – from bedding to theatre gowns to towels – wouldn’t get to our customers on time without the efforts of our people.

So a big thank you to our night shift team, who have shown great patience and good humour as they’ve worked with us to change the way we work and get to grips with the new equipment. We wouldn’t have achieved the results we have without you.

There’s still more to do, but it’s worth celebrating our progress.

Thanks to you all!
