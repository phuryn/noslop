Pre-paid funeral plans aren’t for everyone An average funeral in Norfolk will cost around £4,200 so by rights a pre paid funeral plan should cover all of this. What many people don’t realise is that a pre paid funeral plan only covers certain services and other things you may want will still need to be paid for. Another issue can be if you need to change your plan at a later date for whatever reason, perhaps you want to change the service, move away, or the plan provider is changing their offering. Getting a refund for a pre paid funeral plan can be quite tricky. For many people setting aside money in a bank account or even just writing down your wishes and talking them through with family can be a better option. We understand that for some people a pre paid funeral plan is the right thing for them and that’s perfectly fine, just make sure you fully understand the details of the plan you are buying. If you want to discuss your options with us we always give people 30 minutes to talk about their planning requirements at no cost to yourself. You don’t have to use our service at the end of the meeting if you don’t want to.
