The Change The change started to come quickly, it wasn't long before I realised that this was what I had been missing in London. I felt that I was truly needed here, I worked for about ten minutes to open a lock for a lady in her 70's who had been locked out of her house on the Bishopthorpe Road for about four months now, it was raining too, it was November and she had been stood on her doorstep for the morning already with her shopping. When I eventually opened it, she forced a flask of tea on me and couldn't stop thanking me for helping her. It was the first time in a while that I wasn't thinking about screens and profit and loss accounts, nobody ever thanked me back in London, even when I was making all that money. I got in the car to go home that day for the first time feeling like I was happy.
