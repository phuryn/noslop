Prepaid funeral plans can feel like a kind and sensible thing to do, and for some families they are. But they aren't right for everyone, and it's worth thinking carefully before you sign. The average funeral in Norfolk costs about £4,200, yet a plan bought today may only cover certain services, leaving extras like flowers, catering, or a venue to be paid for later. Plans can also be hard to change if your wishes shift, you move away, or the provider's terms alter, and getting a refund isn't always simple. Some people are better served by putting money aside in an account they control, or by writing down their wishes and talking them through with family. None of this means a plan is wrong for you, only that it deserves a close look at the small print first.
