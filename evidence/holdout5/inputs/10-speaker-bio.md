With 15 years of experience, they are a consultant prosthetist at Limbwise in Sheffield. Their talk at the ISPO UK conference on 12 November, “Fitting Children Who Keep Growing”, will explore the practical challenges of fitting prostheses for children as they grow. Growth brings changing needs, making fit an ongoing consideration rather than a single appointment. Drawing on their clinical experience, they will discuss what this means for prosthetic care and reflect on the decisions involved in supporting children through those changes, with comfort, function and everyday life at the centre of the conversation.
