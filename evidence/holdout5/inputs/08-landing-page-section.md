A desk for you, a crèche spot for your little one—all in one booking on MacCurtain Street.

### 1. Pick your half day
Choose a morning session from 9am–1pm or an afternoon session from 1pm–5pm. Book your desk and childcare slot together, so there’s no need to arrange them separately.

### 2. Come along together
Bring your little one to the crèche, then settle in at your desk. You’ll both be in the same place, with time set aside for work and care.

### 3. Make time for your work
Get four hours to focus on whatever needs your attention. Each half day costs €28, including your desk and the crèche, so you know the price before you book.
