Since March, our Avonmouth plant has been running a new tunnel washer, and the difference in water use has been bigger than we expected.

We now process 14 tonnes of hospital linen every day, and we're doing it with far less water than before. For a site this size, that matters. It's better for the environment, it helps with running costs, and it means we can keep up with demand without the same strain on resources.

None of this happens just because a machine arrives on site. Bedding, theatre gowns and towels need to be clean, safe and back on the ward on time, and that takes people who care about getting it right.

A special thank you to our night shift team. You've adapted to new equipment, new routines and new ways of working, and you've done it with patience and good humour, often while most of us are asleep. The results we're seeing are down to you.

There's more to do, and we'll keep looking for ways to improve. But it's good to pause and recognise progress when it happens.

Thank you to everyone involved.
