Apple Day at the farm is on Saturday 18 October, 11am to 4pm. We'll be out among the orchards all day, celebrating apples.

There'll be pressing demos, so you can watch fresh fruit turn into juice from start to finish. We're also pouring our new perry, Quiet Field, for the first time, which means you'll be among the very first to taste it.

Entry is free, so come for an hour or stay the whole afternoon. Bring family, friends and an appetite. We'd love to see you there 🍎
