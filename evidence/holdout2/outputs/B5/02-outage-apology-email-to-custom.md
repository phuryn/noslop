Subject: We're sorry you were left without internet

Hi,

You were without internet on Tuesday 30 September, and we're sorry. The outage affected around 4,000 homes in Llanidloes and the surrounding area and lasted about nine hours. That's a long time to be cut off when you're trying to work, keep in touch or relax at home, and we let you down.

Everyone affected will get a £15 credit on their next bill. You don't need to contact us or apply for it.

Thanks for your patience.
