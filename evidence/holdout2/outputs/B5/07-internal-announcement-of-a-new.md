Subject: Near miss reporting: new 24-hour requirement from 1 November

Hi all,

From 1 November, every near miss on site has to be logged in the SiteSafe app within 24 hours of it happening.

A near miss is any event that could have caused injury, damage or harm but didn't. If you're not sure whether something counts, log it. The 24 hours start when the event happens, so don't wait for a convenient moment or for someone else to report it. This applies to everyone on site, including subcontractors and their teams.

Near misses are our early warning. Prompt, accurate reports let us fix problems before someone gets hurt, but only if we hear about them while the details are fresh. Logging one isn't about blame. It's how we look out for each other.

Before 1 November, please make sure the SiteSafe app is installed on your phone and that you can log in. If you have any trouble, speak to your site manager. Subcontractor supervisors, please make sure your teams know about the requirement and have access to the app. And log every near miss within 24 hours, without exception.

Next week, site managers will run a 15-minute briefing. We expect you to attend. You'll see how to log a report, and you can ask questions.

Thanks for your help in getting this right.
