In January, driving home after a night shift, I saw a barn owl over the A47. By the end of the year, I'd seen 187 species of bird. That seems a slightly excessive response to an owl.

I'd like to say I spent the year becoming more attentive to the natural world. I did, sometimes. I also spent quite a lot of it trying to work out whether a distant bird was something new or a bird I'd already seen, standing at an unhelpful angle.

The owl was a good place to start. I didn't have a list then, or much idea that I wanted one. I was tired, on my way home, and there it was. I've since put considerably more effort into seeing birds that made less of an impression.
