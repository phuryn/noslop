**Subject:** Near miss reporting: new 24-hour requirement from 1 November

Hi all,

From 1 November, every near miss on site must be logged in the SiteSafe app within 24 hours of it happening.

What counts
A near miss is any event that could have caused injury, damage or harm but didn't. If you're unsure whether something counts, log it. The 24 hours start when the event happens, so don't wait for a convenient moment or for someone else to report it. This applies to everyone on site, including subcontractors and their teams.

Why we're doing this
Near misses are our early warning. Prompt, accurate reports let us fix problems before someone gets hurt, and that only works if we hear about them while the details are fresh. Logging a near miss is not about blame.

What you need to do
- Before 1 November, make sure SiteSafe is installed and you can log in. If you have any trouble, speak to your site manager.
- Subcontractor supervisors: make sure your teams know about this requirement and have access to the app.
- Log every near miss within 24 hours, without exception.

Briefing
Site managers will run a 15-minute briefing next week where you can see how to log a report and ask questions. Attendance is expected.
