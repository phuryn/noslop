Apple Day at the farm: Saturday 18 October, 11am to 4pm.

Spend the day among the orchards. We'll be pressing fresh fruit into juice, and you can watch it from start to finish. We're also pouring Quiet Field, our new perry, for the first time, so you'll be among the first to taste it.

Entry is free. Come for an hour or stay the whole afternoon, and bring family and friends.
