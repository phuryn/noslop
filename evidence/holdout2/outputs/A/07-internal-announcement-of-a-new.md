Subject: Near miss reporting: new 24-hour requirement from 1 November

Hi all,

From 1 November, every near miss on site must be logged in the SiteSafe app within 24 hours of it happening.

What this means
- A near miss is any event that could have caused injury or damage but didn't. If you're unsure whether something counts, log it.
- The 24-hour clock starts when the event happens. Don't wait for a convenient moment or for someone else to report it.
- This applies to everyone on site, including subcontractors and their teams.

Why we're doing this
Near miss reports let us fix problems before someone gets hurt, but only if we hear about them while the details are fresh. Logging one isn't about blame.

What you need to do
- Make sure the SiteSafe app is installed and you can log in before 1 November. If you have any trouble, speak to your site manager.
- Subcontractor supervisors: please make sure your teams know about this requirement and have access to the app.
- Log every near miss within 24 hours.

Briefing
Site managers will run a 15-minute briefing on this next week. Attendance is expected. You'll see how to log a report and can ask questions.
