**Subject: Near miss reporting: new 24-hour requirement from 1 November**

Hi all,

From **1 November**, every near miss on site must be logged in the SiteSafe app within **24 hours** of it happening.

**What this means**
- A near miss is any event that could have caused injury, damage or harm but didn't. If you're unsure whether something counts, log it.
- The 24-hour clock starts when the event happens. Don't wait for a convenient moment or for someone else to report it.
- This applies to everyone on site, including subcontractors and their teams.

**Why we're doing this**
Near misses are our early warning. Prompt, accurate reports let us fix problems before someone gets hurt, and they only work if we hear about them while the details are fresh. Logging a near miss is not about blame. It's how we keep each other safe.

**What you need to do**
- Make sure you have the SiteSafe app installed and can log in before 1 November. If you have any trouble, speak to your site manager.
- Subcontractor supervisors: please make sure your teams know about this requirement and have access to the app.
- Log every near miss within 24 hours, without exception.

**Briefing**
Site managers will run a 15-minute briefing on this next week. Attendance is expected, and it's a chance to see how to log a report and ask questions.

Thanks for your support in getting this right.
