Subject: We’re sorry you were left without internet

Hi,

We’re sorry you were without internet on Tuesday 30 September. The outage affected around 4,000 homes in Llanidloes and the surrounding area and lasted about nine hours.

That’s a long time to be cut off, whether you were trying to work, keep in touch or relax at home. We know it disrupted your day, and we let you down.

Everyone affected will receive a £15 credit on their next bill. You don’t need to contact us or apply for it.

Thank you for your patience. We’re sorry for the trouble this caused.
